What is Sunday League?

  • Sunday League is a way for community members to organize intermural sports leagues, and get to know the athletes you're playing with.
  • Registered users can join teams, or create teams of their own.
  • Registered users can create a sports league for a wide variety of sports.
  • Team admins can sign their team up to join a league, and participate in games scheduled by the league admin.
  • Members of a league can post pictures and messages whenever, and get to know each other.
  • Members of the same team can reward each other with upvotes for their skills during a match.
